Collaboration

Invite your team

Each teammate uses their own account (the email you invite). Do not give them your username or password. If they log in as you, they are you — they will see every project you own.

How they join

  1. You pick a project role and send the email or copy the accept link.
  2. They open the email link and choose their own password on that page. That signs them in. They must not use your username.
  3. If they already registered with that email, they are added as soon as you invite (no extra click).

What each project role can do

Role Sees Can do Cannot do
Developer Only projects they joined View findings, edit files, run scans Invite people, delete the project, change CI
Analyst Only projects they joined View findings and run scans Edit files, invite people, delete the project
Project admin Only projects they joined Everything a developer can, plus invite/manage members and CI Delete the project (owner only)
Owner (you) Projects you created, plus any you were invited to Full control, including delete —

Sidebar Admin is a platform operator account, not the project-admin invite role. Invited admins do not get that page.
